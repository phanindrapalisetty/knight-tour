# v0.4.0

Released 29 October 2026 · Tag `v0.4.0` · Branch `release/v0.4.0`

The web version now works like an app: install it, and play with no connection.

## What's new

- **Play offline.** After your first visit, the whole game is saved on your device, fonts included. It opens and plays with no internet connection.
- **Update prompt.** When a new version is out, a message says "A new version is available." Tap **Reload** to switch; your game in progress is saved first. Tap ✕ to keep playing and update later.
- **"Ready to play offline."** A short message confirms when the game has been saved for offline use.
- **Better install dialog.** On Android and desktop Chrome, installing now shows screenshots of the game.

## For developers

- The placeholder `public/sw.js` is replaced by a service worker that `vite-plugin-pwa` (Workbox) generates at build time. It precaches the build (23 files, about 380 KB) and leaves out the install screenshots.
- The manifest stays our own, generated from `app.config.js`; it now has an `id` and `screenshots`.
- `src/pwa.js` registers the worker and shows the prompts. It checks for updates whenever the app comes back into view and hourly while it's open.
- The service worker is skipped inside the future native apps (`Capacitor.isNativePlatform()`), which bundle their files.
- Install screenshots are in `public/screenshots/`.
- New dev dependencies: `vite-plugin-pwa`, `workbox-build`, `workbox-window`. `npm audit` reports 4 low-severity issues inside `workbox-build`. They're build-time only and never reach players, and the only suggested fix is a downgrade to years-old versions.

## Known limitations

- Not yet tried on a real Android phone or iPhone.
- Android and iOS apps are still to come.
