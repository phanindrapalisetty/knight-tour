# v0.2.0

Released 29 October 2026 · Tag `v0.2.0` · Branch `release/v0.2.0`

A tidier Home screen: the look-and-feel settings now live in a menu.

## What's new

- **Menu.** A ☰ button at the top right of Home opens a panel with **Board theme** and **Appearance** (System, Light, Dark). Changes apply right away, so you can compare themes without closing it.
- **Shorter Home.** With the settings moved, Home fits on most phone screens without scrolling.
- **Version shown.** The app name and version appear at the bottom of the menu.

## Closing the menu

Tap ✕, tap outside the panel, press Esc, or use the back button. Your place on Home is kept.

## Accessibility

- Opening the menu moves focus into it, and closing it returns focus to the ☰ button.
- Tab and Shift+Tab stay inside the open menu.
- After any change on screen, keyboard focus stays on the control you were using.

## For developers

- New `src/screens/menu.js`; the menu is the `#/menu` route, drawn over Home.
- The version in the menu is read from `package.json`.
- Branches are now named after the release they carry, e.g. `release/v0.2.0`.
